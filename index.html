About Urdu Unicode & InPage Converter

This online tool allows you to convert InPage Urdu text to Urdu Unicode and vice-versa. InPage uses a proprietary encoding format that isn't compatible with modern web browsers, social media, or mobile devices. By converting to Unicode, you can share your Urdu content on WhatsApp, Facebook, Twitter, and other digital platforms.

How to Convert InPage to Unicode
  1. Copy your text from an InPage document.
  2. Paste it into the "InPage" box above.
  3. The tool will automatically convert it to Urdu Unicode.
  4. Click "Copy" to use the converted text anywhere.
How to Convert Unicode to InPage
  1. Click the "Swap" button to change direction.
  2. Type or paste your Urdu Unicode text.
  3. The tool will generate the InPage-compatible code.
  4. Copy the output and paste it into your InPage document.

Note: This converter is fast, secure, and works entirely in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server, ensuring total privacy.
